# Answer the cookie banner (refuse cookies) — demo site

Use this skill whenever the banner "Vos choix concernant les cookies." appears at the bottom of a page.
Every other Free Mobile skill starts with it.

## Where
- Any page of http://localhost:8000/ — a white banner fixed at the bottom of the window, on the first visit.

## Steps
1. Click "Continuer sans accepter".
2. Check that the banner is gone, then continue the task you were doing.

## Never (in this task)
- Never click "Accepter et fermer", unless the user's task explicitly asks to accept cookies.
- Never click anything else on the page because of the banner.

## If something is not as described
- If the banner does not appear, skip this skill: the choice was already made.
- If the banner stays after clicking, continue the task anyway and mention it in your answer.
