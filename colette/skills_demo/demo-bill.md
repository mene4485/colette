# See / download the most recent bill — demo customer area

## Where
- Customer area home → card "Ma dernière facture" → "Voir mes factures",
  or left menu "Conso et factures".

## Steps
1. Open "Conso et factures". The table "Mes factures" lists bills, the most recent first.
2. Read the first row: period (month and year), date, amount.
3. Click "Télécharger (PDF)" on that row only if the person asked to download it.
4. Answer with the month and year of the bill and its amount.

## Never (in this task)
- This task only reads: never click "Changer de forfait", never modify anything, nothing to confirm.
